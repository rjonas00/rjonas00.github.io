About Me

My name is Raffael Jonas (25), based in Vienna. I am currently in my Master’s program in the Astrophysics Department at the University of Vienna. As a research staff member within the Data Science & Cosmology Group of Prof. Oliver Hahn, I am actively participating in frontier research regarding cosmic structure formation.

I am especially interested in structure growth and physical and empirical models to understand the connection between dark matter and resulting baryonic structures. I specialize in the statistical analysis of density fields, power spectra, and higher-order statistics (bispectra and trispectra).

Another core interest of mine is the Dark Ages and Reionization, using neutral media as a tracer of structure formation, alongside wavelet analysis for time-dependent signals.

To summarize: I love any type of data analysis to gain understanding, using statistical and intuitive visualization to find new ways of thinking about complex problem sets.

About Me

My name is Raffael Jonas (25), based in Vienna. I am currently a Master’s student in the Astrophysics Department at the University of Vienna. As a research staff member within the Data Science & Cosmology Group of Prof. Oliver Hahn, I am actively participating in frontier research regarding cosmic structure formation.

I am primarily interested in structure growth and the development of physical and empirical models to decode the connection between dark matter and resulting baryonic structures. To gain this understanding, I specialize in the statistical analysis of density fields, specifically utilizing power spectra, correlation functions, and higher-order statistics such as bispectra and trispectra.

Another core interest of mine lies in the early eras of the universe, specifically the Dark Ages and Reionization. I am interested in the prospect of using neutral media to probe the connection between gas and dark matter as a tracer of structure formation. Technically, I am fascinated by the application of wavelets and the analysis of time-dependent signals of emission and absorption.

I love using data analysis and machine learning to find new ways of thinking about complex problems. My approach combines rigorous statistical methods with intuitive physical understanding and visualization to gain deep insights.

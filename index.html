About Me

I am a Master’s student in Astrophysics at the University of Vienna. As a member of the Data Science & Cosmology Group under Prof. Oliver Hahn, I contribute to frontier research in structure growth and the complex connection between dark matter and baryonic structures.

My research focuses on the statistical analysis of density fields, utilizing power spectra, correlation functions, and higher-order statistics (bispectra and trispectra) to gain a deeper understanding of cosmic evolution.

I am especially interested in the Dark Ages and Reionization, using neutral media as a tracer for structure formation. Furthermore, I apply wavelet analysis to time-dependent signals to find new ways of interpreting data through statistical intuition and visualization.
